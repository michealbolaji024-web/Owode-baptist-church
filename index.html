WHO WE ARE

A church family centered on Christ

Owode Baptist Church, Ogbomoso is a Christian community committed to worship, discipleship, evangelism, fellowship and service.

We believe that every person matters to God. Our desire is to create a welcoming environment where children, youths, adults and families can grow in faith and use their gifts to serve God and others.

LoveServing people with Christ-like love.
TruthGrowing through God's Word.
FellowshipBuilding a strong church family.
MissionSharing the Gospel with others.
